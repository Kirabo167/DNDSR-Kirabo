# Tsinghua doctoral-thesis format defaults (2026)

Use these values only when the current university, department, or user requirements
do not override them. They were distilled from the September 2026 review/writing
requirements, the May 2026 Word examples, and ThuThesis v7.7.1.

## Page and body

| Item | Default |
|---|---|
| Paper | A4 portrait, 210 mm × 297 mm |
| Margins | 3.0 cm on all four sides; gutter 0 |
| Header/footer distance | 2.2 cm from page edge |
| Chinese body | SimSun, 12 pt |
| Latin letters/numbers | Times New Roman, 12 pt |
| Paragraph | Justified; first-line indent 2 Chinese characters |
| Body spacing | 0 pt before/after; exactly 20 pt |

For a standalone body chapter, use Arabic page numbers starting at 1. A full thesis
uses uppercase Roman page numbers for front matter from the Chinese abstract, then
Arabic numbers for the main text.

## Heading hierarchy

| Level | Format |
|---|---|
| Chapter | SimHei/Arial 16 pt, centered, single; 24 pt before, 18 pt after |
| Section | SimHei/Arial 14 pt, left, exactly 20 pt; 24 pt before, 6 pt after |
| Subsection | SimHei/Arial 13 pt, left, exactly 20 pt; 12 pt before, 6 pt after |
| Fourth level | SimHei/Arial 12 pt, left, exactly 20 pt; 12 pt before, 6 pt after |

Use `第1章　标题`, `1.1　标题`, `1.1.1　标题`; separate number and title by one
full-width space. Begin each chapter on a new page and keep headings with the first
following paragraph.

## Header and footer

- Header: current chapter number and title, centered, SimSun 10.5 pt.
- Header bottom rule: 0.75 pt single line.
- Footer: centered Arabic page number, Times New Roman 10.5 pt.

## Tables, figures, and equations

- Figure/table captions: SimSun/Times New Roman 11 pt, centered, no colon.
- Table caption above table: 12 pt before, 6 pt after.
- Figure caption below figure: 6 pt before, 12 pt after.
- Table text: 11 pt, single-spaced, 3 pt before and after.
- Three-line table: top/bottom rules 1.5 pt, header rule 1 pt, no vertical rules.
- Keep a table on one page when practical; never split a single row.
- Figure axes require quantity names and units; use Chinese labels except established
  symbols, codes, or units.
- Equation: centered, chapter number right-aligned in full-width parentheses, e.g.
  `（3-2）`; use one numbering separator consistently.
- Equation paragraph: single-spaced, 6 pt before and after.

## References and notes

- GB/T 7714—2015; choose numeric or author–year once and use it throughout.
- Engineering default: superscript bracketed numeric citations.
- Bibliography: SimSun/Times New Roman 10.5 pt; exactly 16 pt; 3 pt before,
  0 pt after; hanging indent about 1 cm.
- Keep one bibliography entry on one page when practical.
- Footnotes: circled number, restart each page; 9 pt, single-spaced, 0 pt before/after,
  hanging indent 1.5 characters; do not split one note across pages.
